Welcome

Fallingbrook Health and Wellness provides Acupuncture, Cosmetic Acupuncture, Registered Massage Therapy, and complementary Traditional Chinese Medicine techniques — all in a calm, in-home clinic setting where care feels personal and unhurried.

Registered Acupuncture

Traditional acupuncture treatments tailored to your individual health goals, from pain management to overall wellness.

Cosmetic Acupuncture

A natural, non-invasive approach to healthy, radiant skin using fine-needle facial acupuncture techniques.

Registered Massage Therapy

Therapeutic massage to relieve tension, reduce stress, and support your body's natural recovery.

TCM Techniques

Cupping, Tui Na (acupressure Chinese massage), Moxibustion, and Gua Sha, used alongside acupuncture as needed.

Over 16 Years in Health Care

After a decade working as a Massage Therapist, our practitioner became a full-time Registered Acupuncturist, holding a diploma in Massage Therapy from Centennial College and diplomas in Acupuncture from the Shiatsu School of Canada and the SSC Acupuncture Institute.
