Policies

Cancellation Policy: If an appointment is cancelled within 24 hours of its start time, you will be billed 25% of the service price. This fee is the responsibility of the patient and cannot be billed to your insurance company.
Late Arrivals: Please arrive on time for your appointment. Arriving late may mean a shortened treatment, as your session will still end at the originally scheduled time out of respect for the next client.
Insurance & Receipts: Receipts suitable for extended health insurance claims are provided after each appointment. Please confirm your coverage for Registered Acupuncture and Registered Massage Therapy directly with your provider.

Questions about any of these policies? Please get in touch and we're happy to help.
