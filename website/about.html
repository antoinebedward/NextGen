About Me

My Background

I graduated from Centennial College with a diploma in Massage Therapy and later earned diplomas in Acupuncture from the Shiatsu School of Canada and the SSC Acupuncture Institute.

After spending a decade working as a Massage Therapist, I transitioned to working full-time as a Registered Acupuncturist. Altogether, I've spent over sixteen years in the health care field, and I bring that experience into every treatment at Fallingbrook Health and Wellness.

I offer Registered Acupuncture, Cosmetic Acupuncture, and Registered Massage Therapy — along with complementary Traditional Chinese Medicine techniques like Cupping, Tui Na, Moxibustion, and Gua Sha — from the comfort of a private, in-home clinic on Princess Louise Drive in Orléans.

My Approach

Every treatment plan is built around you: your health history, your goals, and how your body responds. I take the time to listen before recommending a course of treatment, and I adjust as we go.

Let's Work Together

Whether you're managing pain, looking to relax, or curious about Traditional Chinese Medicine, I'd love to help.
