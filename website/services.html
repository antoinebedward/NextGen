What Each Treatment Involves

Acupuncture

Fine, sterile needles are placed at specific points to relieve pain, reduce stress, and support the body's own healing processes.

Cosmetic Acupuncture

A gentle, natural alternative to invasive cosmetic procedures, using facial needling to promote circulation and skin health.

Registered Massage Therapy

Hands-on therapeutic massage to release muscle tension, improve circulation, and support recovery from injury or stress.

Cupping

Suction cups are applied to the skin to improve blood flow and ease muscle tension, often used alongside acupuncture.

Tui Na

A traditional Chinese acupressure massage technique that works along the body's energy meridians.

Moxibustion & Gua Sha

Supportive Traditional Chinese Medicine techniques using heat (moxibustion) or gentle scraping (Gua Sha) to encourage healing.
